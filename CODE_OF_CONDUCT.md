# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Be straightforward and be kind. Those are compatible.

This project is about telling people uncomfortable truths — that their evidence
is weak, that their change is out of scope, that a passing test proved less than
they hoped. Doing that well requires directness. It never requires contempt.

## Expected

- Critique the work, the evidence, and the argument.
- Say what you actually think, including when it is unwelcome.
- Assume the other person is capable and acting in good faith.
- Change your position when the evidence changes, and say so plainly.
- Accept that "I was wrong" is a normal sentence and not a defeat.

## Not accepted

- Personal attacks, demeaning language, or contempt for someone's competence.
- Harassment, sexual attention, or unwelcome personal remarks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption, bad-faith argument, or deliberately wasting others' time.
- Using directness as cover for cruelty. The distinction is whether you are
  attacking the work or the person, and it is rarely ambiguous.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, code review, and any space
where you are identifiably participating in this project.

## Reporting

Report through GitHub's private reporting for this repository, or contact the
repository owner through their verified GitHub profile. Reports are handled
privately.

Include what happened, where, and when. Links help. You do not need to prove a
pattern, and you do not need to be the person it happened to.

## Enforcement

The maintainer responds. In order of severity: a private clarification, a public
correction, a temporary block, or a permanent ban.

Two commitments. Enforcement will not depend on how valuable a contributor is —
a prolific contributor who is cruel is a net loss. And the reasoning will be
stated, because unexplained moderation is indistinguishable from arbitrary
moderation.

## A note on scale

This project currently has one maintainer. That means enforcement is one
person's judgement, which is a real limitation and worth knowing. If the project
grows, this section should be replaced with something less dependent on a single
person.
